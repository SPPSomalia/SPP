A party of programme, not personality

\
\
\

For more than thirty years, Somalis have carried this country themselves. When the\ state collapsed, families fed the hungry, traders kept goods moving, teachers taught in\ the ruins, and Somalis abroad sent home the money that kept millions alive. Whatever\ recovery Somalia has achieved was achieved first by its people.

\

That recovery is real, and we honour those who delivered it. But our politics is still\ settled by bargaining among a small number of people, in a small number of rooms, in the\ name of clans who are never asked. The young are the overwhelming majority of this nation,\ and they are told to wait. Many stop waiting and leave.

\

A country cannot be built on a politics that excludes its own majority. The SPP was\ founded to change how Somali politics works \'97 not merely who holds office within it. We\ compete for power in order to deliver a published plan, and we accept the only discipline\ that makes democracy real.

\
\
\ \
\
\ \
\
The canopy
\
The shade tree under which Somali communities have gathered for centuries to\ deliberate and decide together. Its breadth is deliberate: this party shelters all\ Somalis, without distinction.
\
The star
\
The five-pointed star of the national flag. We are a national party, loyal to the\ Somali state and its Constitution, and the unity of the Somali people stands above the\ interest of any clan, region or faction.
\
The trunk and base
\
One root dividing into two arms that hold the canopy up \'97 the federal government and\ the member states, the state and the citizen. Neither holds it alone. The base beneath\ them is the Constitution and the rule of law.

The programme

\

Ten pillars, each with commitments we expect to be measured against.\ Open any pillar to read what we have undertaken to do.

\ \
\ \
\

Peace, security and the rule of law

\

Somali forces, lawfully commanded and paid on time \'97 and policing, courts\ and administration in every district we recover.

\
    \
  • Fund and complete the National Security Architecture on the agreed timelines, with quarterly public reporting.
    • \
  • Pay every soldier and police officer directly and on time, on a biometric payroll, with published audit results.
    • \
  • Place police, a functioning court and a district administration in every recovered district within 90 days.
    • \
  • Build a properly federated police service with common standards for training, vetting and human rights compliance.
    • \
  • Establish independent civilian oversight of the security sector, with real investigative powers.
    • \
  • Rebuild the Coast Guard to protect our fisheries and our exclusive economic zone.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Constitution, federalism and elections

\

Finish the constitutional settlement by negotiation, and put every office\ to a vote.

\
    \
  • Complete the constitutional review with the Federal Member States, both Houses, women's organisations, youth, scholars and the diaspora.
    • \
  • Settle the allocation of powers and the sharing of revenue in law, with formula-based transfers rather than discretionary favour.
    • \
  • Deliver one person, one vote \'97 beginning at district level and building to national elections on a funded timetable.
    • \
  • Fully fund and protect the independence of the NIEBC, with security for polling and full observer accreditation.
    • \
  • Resolve the status of Banadir by negotiated agreement, so the capital's residents hold the same rights as other Somalis.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Integrity and accountable government

\

Public office is a trust. We will make that verifiable rather than\ declare it.

\
    \
  • Establish an independent Anti-Corruption Commission with power to investigate any officeholder.
    • \
  • Require published, verified annual asset declarations from the President, Prime Minister, Ministers, MPs, State Presidents and judges.
    • \
  • Publish every public contract above a threshold together with the beneficial ownership of the contracting company.
    • \
  • Consolidate revenue into a single treasury account and digitise customs and tax collection.
    • \
  • Protect whistleblowers and journalists in law, and end the detention of journalists for their reporting.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Economy, jobs and livelihoods

\

Somali enterprise is the engine. The state's job is roads, power, courts\ and rules that are the same for everyone.

\
    \
  • Enact a modern commercial legal framework, with commercial courts that resolve disputes to a published deadline.
    • \
  • Rebuild veterinary services, quarantine and export certification so Somali livestock commands a better price.
    • \
  • Develop fisheries as a national industry: licensing, cold chain, landing sites, processing, and enforcement against illegal foreign fishing.
    • \
  • Invest in irrigation, river management and flood control along the Shabelle and Jubba.
    • \
  • Launch a National Youth Employment Programme and an SME credit guarantee facility, with dedicated windows for women- and youth-owned businesses.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Education

\

Free primary schooling, teachers who are actually paid, and one\ certificate that means the same everywhere in Somalia.

\
    \
  • Phase out fees in public primary schools and run a national enrolment drive for out-of-school children.
    • \
  • Prioritise girls' enrolment and retention through school feeding, safe sanitation and stipends where cost is the barrier.
    • \
  • Recruit, train, register and pay teachers properly, under a national qualification and licensing framework.
    • \
  • Establish a unified national curriculum and certification, and integrate Qur'anic and dugsi education with pathways into formal schooling.
    • \
  • Expand technical and vocational training in the trades the economy actually needs.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Health and social protection

\

An essential package of care, free at the point of use, starting with\ mothers and children.

\
    \
  • Deliver free maternal, newborn and child health, immunisation, nutrition and communicable disease services in public facilities.
    • \
  • Put a skilled birth attendant within reach of every community, with functioning emergency obstetric referral.
    • \
  • Rebuild the primary health care network district by district, with reliable supply of essential medicines.
    • \
  • Establish medicines regulation to end the circulation of counterfeit and expired drugs.
    • \
  • Introduce cash transfers to the poorest households, people with disabilities and orphans, with published beneficiary registers.
    • \
  • Deliver durable solutions for displaced Somalis \'97 secure tenure, services and livelihoods, with protection against forced eviction.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Youth and women

\

The majority of this country is young, and half of it is women. It cannot\ keep being governed as though neither were true.

\
    \
  • Entrench a minimum 30% representation of women in elected and appointed office in law \'97 applied inside our own party first.
    • \
  • Reserve a meaningful share of appointed public positions for Somalis under 35, with figures published annually.
    • \
  • Establish a National Youth Council with statutory standing and a real budget, elected by young people rather than appointed.
    • \
  • Enforce laws against sexual and gender-based violence, with specialised police units and survivor-centred services.
    • \
  • Support women's economic participation through dedicated credit, market infrastructure and property rights enforcement.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Infrastructure, energy and water

\

Cheaper power, roads that reach the ports, and water that survives a dry\ year.

\
    \
  • Build and rehabilitate the corridors connecting production areas to ports, markets and each other.
    • \
  • Cut the cost of electricity \'97 among the highest in the world \'97 through regulation and large-scale solar and wind.
    • \
  • Deliver water security through boreholes, berkeds, dams and urban systems, under a national water authority for the Jubba and Shabelle basins.
    • \
  • Extend affordable internet to underserved regions and digitise government services.
    • \
  • Establish a land registry and a working land dispute mechanism \'97 unresolved land conflict is among the most dangerous unaddressed issues in Somalia.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Climate and resilience

\

Somalia did not cause this. We will stop responding to it as though it\ were a surprise.

\
    \
  • Shift from emergency response to resilience, with strategic grain and fodder reserves and pre-positioned response capacity.
    • \
  • Build early warning and disaster management with the authority and budget to act before a crisis becomes a famine.
    • \
  • End the charcoal trade through enforcement combined with credible alternative livelihoods and affordable fuels.
    • \
  • Launch a national reforestation and rangeland restoration programme, employing young people at scale.
    • \
  • Claim Somalia's share of international climate finance through a credible adaptation plan and the capacity to account for it.
    • \
\
\ \
\

Foreign policy and the diaspora

\

A partner to many and a client of none \'97 and a formal place in national\ life for Somalis abroad.

\
    \
  • Pursue a sovereign, non-aligned foreign policy, and refuse to let Somalia become an arena for other states' rivalries.
    • \
  • Rebuild a professional diplomatic service recruited on merit and resourced to represent Somali interests.
    • \
  • Deepen regional integration through the East African Community, IGAD and the African Union, prioritising trade and labour mobility.
    • \
  • Give the diaspora voting rights on a defined and secure basis, streamlined investment channels and recognition of foreign qualifications.
    • \
  • Move the international partnership from aid towards trade and investment, aligned to a published national development plan.
    • \

What we will do in the first 100 days

\

If entrusted with government, these are the actions by which our first\ three months should be judged.

\
    \
  1. Publish a costed implementation plan for this programme, with named responsible ministries and quarterly milestones.
    2. \
  2. Publish asset declarations for the President, Prime Minister and every member of Cabinet.
    3. \
  3. Submit legislation to Parliament establishing the independent Anti-Corruption Commission.
    4. \
  4. Publish a funded, dated electoral roadmap agreed with the NIEBC and the Federal Member States.
    5. \
  5. Convene a Federal\'96State Leaders' Forum on powers, revenue sharing and constitutional completion.
    6. \
  6. Order a full biometric audit of the security forces payroll and begin direct monthly payment.
    7. \
  7. Announce the phased abolition of public primary school fees and open the national enrolment drive.
    8. \
  8. Publish the national budget and all off-budget revenue in Somali, in accessible format.
    9. \
  9. Establish the National Youth Council and open the first cohort of the youth employment programme.
    10. \
  10. Table the legislative package on women's representation and on sexual and gender-based violence.
    11. \

Our covenant with the Somali people

\

We do not ask you to trust us because of who we are, where we come from, or\ who our fathers were. We ask you to judge us against what we have written down.

\
    \
  • On clanWe will not campaign on clan, and we will not govern by clan.
    • \
  • On the recordWe will publish what we promise, and report on what we deliver.
    • \
  • On public moneyWe will not use public office to enrich ourselves or our families.
    • \
  • On powerWe will accept the verdict of the ballot box, and hand over power peacefully when the people decide.

Become a member

\

Membership is open to any Somali citizen aged 18 or above who accepts the\ Party Constitution, Manifesto and Code of Conduct, and who is not a member of another\ registered party.

\

Members vote in internal elections, stand for party office, select our\ candidates in primaries, and receive the party's audited accounts.
